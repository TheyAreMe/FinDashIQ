## 1. Educational & Research Purpose Disclaimer

FinDashIQ is developed and provided **strictly for educational, software demonstration, and algorithmic research purposes**. The platform is designed to showcase multi-factor technical analysis, mathematical indicator modeling, and natural language synthesis architectures.

## 2. No Investment or Financial Advice

- **Non-Advisory Nature**: No content, quantitative conviction score, probabilistic scenario, backtest simulation result, execution matrix setup, or AI synthesis on FinDashIQ constitutes financial, investment, legal, tax, or trading advice.
- **No Brokerage Services**: FinDashIQ is an analytical software interface and does not execute real-money trades, hold client funds, or act as an exchange, broker-dealer, or registered investment advisor (RIA).
- **Independent Due Diligence**: Users must conduct their own independent financial research and consult with certified financial professionals before executing real-world investments.

## 5. Data Privacy & GDPR Compliance

FinDashIQ is built on a privacy-first, self-hosted architecture:
- **Local Data Storage**: All user account credentials, watchlists, preferences, and alert rules are stored exclusively on your server instance in the `data/` directory.
- **Zero Tracking Cookies**: The platform does not use third-party analytics trackers, advertising pixels, or telemetry beacons.
- **Session Security**: Session tokens are protected via standard HTTP cookie security flags and hardened headers (`HSTS`, `CSP`, `X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff`).
