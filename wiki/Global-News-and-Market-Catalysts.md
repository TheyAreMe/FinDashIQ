## 1. Overview & Wire Coverage

FinDashIQ ingests breaking news from leading financial newsrooms and wire services globally:
- **Tier 1 Global Financial Media**: Reuters, Bloomberg, CNBC, MarketWatch, Finviz.
- **European & International Media**: Handelsblatt, Tagesschau, Nikkei Asia, Agence France-Presse, and regional market wire feeds.
- **Corporate Filings & Press Releases**: Earnings reports, SEC 8-K / 10-Q disclosures, FDA clearances, and executive leadership announcements.

---

## 2. Global News Modal Features

Open the news stream by clicking **"Breaking News"** in the top navigation or the news trigger on any stock header:

- **Universal Search**: Filter news articles in real-time by keyword, publisher name, or asset ticker.
- **Category Filter Tabs**:
  - `All Wires`: Complete chronological stream across all international sources.
  - `🚀 High Impact`: Filters for high-conviction catalysts (earnings surprises, regulatory approvals, major contract awards, macro interest rate decisions).
  - `🏛️ Tier 1 Sources`: Restricts feed exclusively to institutional media (Reuters, Bloomberg, CNBC).
- **Direct Source Links**: Click any article headline to open the original source article in a new tab.

---

## 3. Multi-Language Feeds & Automatic Translation

To ensure comprehensive global market coverage for non-US assets (e.g. German, French, Japanese, or Scandinavian equities):
- Foreign-language headlines and abstracts are automatically normalized and translated into English for seamless cross-market analysis.
- Source language badges indicate the original reporting origin.

---

## 4. AI Catalyst Scoring & Sentiment Integration

Breaking news events are not just displayed—they directly influence quantitative analytics:
- **Sentiment Weighting**: AI conviction scores factor in corporate news sentiment to dampen false buy signals during severe negative developments.
- **Catalyst Synthesis**: The AI engine generates dynamic executive summaries highlighting whether news tailwinds validate or invalidate technical breakout patterns.
- **Collision Avoidance**: Multi-exchange ticker disambiguation ensures that corporate events are accurately matched to the target company.

---

## 5. Ticker & Company News Attribution

When inspecting a specific stock in the Deep-Dive Terminal or Watchlist Hub:
- FinDashIQ filters the wire to show only relevant corporate disclosures and industry-specific catalysts for that company.
- Recent catalysts appear directly in the **AI News Catalyst Synthesis** module on the asset's card and terminal overview.
