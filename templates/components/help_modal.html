🧪 Quantitative Strategy Backtesting

Simulate algorithmic execution strategies on historical market data and benchmark performance against a $10,000 Buy & Hold investment across multiple selectable timeframes (1M, 3M, 6M, 1Y, 2Y, 5Y, MAX).

Strategy Types
  • Multi-Factor Quant: Combines SuperTrend trend confirmation, Chaikin Money Flow accumulation, and RSI momentum filters.
  • SuperTrend Breakout: Follows trailing volatility breakout bands to capture major trend expansions while cutting downside risk.
  • MACD + RSI Momentum: Mean-reversion strategy triggering on MACD signal line crosses exiting oversold RSI zones.
Scorecard Metrics
  • Alpha (Excess Return): Total Strategy Return (%) minus Buy & Hold Benchmark (%).
  • Win Rate: Percentage of profitable completed round-trip trades.
  • Profit Factor: Ratio of gross profits to gross losses.
  • Maximum Drawdown: Peak-to-trough capital drawdown during the backtest period.
  • Order Execution Ledger: Comprehensive trade-by-trade log with date, type, price, volume, realized PnL, and trigger reason.

📱 Telegram Bot Setup & Delivery Methods

Receive instant markdown-formatted market catalyst signals, price breakout alerts, and direct news links right in your Telegram chats, public channels, or private trading groups.

🔑 Step 1: Create Bot & Set Token in .env
  1. Open Telegram, search for @BotFather, send /newbot, and follow prompts.
  2. Copy the Bot API Token and add to your .env file: TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN=...
👤 Method A: Direct Private Messages (DMs to You)
  • Step 1: In Telegram, search for your bot (e.g. @FinDashIQ_bot) and tap "Start" (or send hello).
  • Step 2: In FinDashIQ Destination, type auto (or your numeric Chat ID from @raw_data_bot).
  • Step 3: Click Trigger Test Message — the alert will arrive directly in your private chat.
📢 Method B: Public Channels & Public Groups (e.g. @FinDashIQ)
  • Step 1 (Make Public): In Telegram group settings ➔ Edit ➔ Group Type ➔ Set to Public ➔ Assign handle (e.g. FinDashIQ).
  • Step 2 (Admin Rights): In Group Administrators, add your bot (e.g. @FinDashIQ_bot) as an Admin with Post Messages rights.
  • Step 3: In FinDashIQ Destination, enter @FinDashIQ and click Trigger Test Message.
🔒 Method C: Private Groups (No public handle)
  • Step 1: Add your bot to the private group as an Administrator.
  • Step 2: In the group chat, send /test @FinDashIQ_bot.
  • Step 3: In FinDashIQ Destination, enter auto (or the numeric group ID starting with -100...) and click Trigger Test Message.
⚠️ Note on chat not found (HTTP 400): Telegram only allows @handle addressing for Public groups/channels. If your group is Private, either set it to Public (Method B) or use auto with Admin rights (Method C).

⚡ Performance Caching & Infrastructure Security

FinDashIQ achieves sub-10ms response times via multi-tier server-side caching and incremental delta synchronization, backed by hardened reverse proxy security headers.

Performance & Cache Highlights
  • Persistent Disk Cache (data/cache/): Historical bars are saved locally in CSV format.
  • Delta Downloads: On reload, the engine downloads only missing recent bars (1–2 days) rather than full multi-year histories, reducing network bandwidth by >98%.
  • Security Headers: Enforces HSTS, restrictive CSP, X-Frame-Options: SAMEORIGIN, X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff, and ProxyFix headers for reverse proxies.
  • Force Refresh: Click "Force Refresh" in any tab to bypass cached data and perform a live query.
