This application is operated as a self-hosted client/server application. All analytical calculations, quantitative indicator formulas (SuperTrend, VWAP, CMF, RSI, Stochastic, MACD), delta caching, and user authentication are executed within the local operating environment of the deploying user.

3. AI Synthesis & LLM Output Disclaimer

Narratives produced by the AI Financial Copilot, AI Market Scanner, Catalyst Synthesizers, and 30-Day Scenario Generators are generated by Large Language Models (such as Google Gemini or OpenAI GPT). LLMs are probabilistic models capable of hallucinations, factual inaccuracies, and unexpected mathematical variations. Always independently verify all figures before making any real-world financial decisions.

4. Data Feeds Accuracy & Delays

Market quotes, historical OHLCV data, fundamentals, and news feeds are sourced via third-party providers (such as Yahoo Finance / yfinance). Data may be delayed, incomplete, inaccurate, or temporarily unavailable. No warranty is made regarding the continuity or correctness of external market feeds.

1. Local-First Privacy Architecture

FinDashIQ is designed with a privacy-by-design philosophy. The application does not track users, run analytics trackers, or phone home to third-party telemetry servers.

2. API Key Security & Client-Side Storage

Your Google Gemini API Key and OpenAI API Key are stored exclusively in your browser's local storage (localStorage). Keys are never committed to disk in shared configuration files or sent to unauthorized servers. They are transmitted solely to the respective provider API endpoint when requesting AI synthesis.

3. Server-Side Data Storage

User profiles, password hashes (salted SHA-256), watchlists, and alert rules are stored locally on the hosting machine in the data/ folder. Only authorized administrators of the deployment have access to these files.
